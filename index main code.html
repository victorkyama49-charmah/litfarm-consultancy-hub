About Litfarm

Practical guidance for sustainable agriculture.

Litfarm Consultancy Hub—meaning Elite Farming—is a professional agricultural consultancy supporting farmers, youth, groups, and organisations with sustainable production knowledge and practical business thinking.

Litfarm Consultancy Hub is led by Brivin Muia, Founder and Chairperson and Agricultural Economist, and Victor Kyama, Co-Founder and Horticulturist. The team brings together agricultural economics, horticulture, agribusiness planning, climate-smart agriculture, and youth empowerment to support farmers and aspiring agripreneurs.

We serve clients in Nakuru County and Makueni County, with remote consultations available across Kenya.
